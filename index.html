PUC PUC

Product Facts
  • Well-known candy with cult status in Bosnia and the region.
  • Famous for its intense popping and fizzing effect in the mouth.
  • Creates a tingling sensation directly on the tongue.
  • Traditionally available in various fruity flavors.
  • Highly popular among children and as a nostalgic snack.
  • A true classic found in local kiosks and supermarkets.
