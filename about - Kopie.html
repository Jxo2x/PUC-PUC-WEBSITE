About PUC PUC Flavors

Explore the Flavors:
🍓 Jagoda Strawberry

The absolute classic! This variety brings the sweet, rich flavor of freshly picked strawberries. It is a fan-favorite, especially among kids, combining a smooth fruity sweetness with an intense, long-lasting popping sensation on the tongue.

🫐 Šumsko Voće Forest Fruits

A wild and refreshing blend of raspberries, blackberries, and blueberries. This flavor offers a slightly tart and deeply rich berry experience. Perfect for those who love a sophisticated, multi-layered fruit punch mixed with explosive fizzing fun.

🍌 Banana Sweet Banana

A delightfully unique flavor in the popping candy lineup! The banana variety delivers a smooth, distinctively creamy, and sweet tropical aroma. Combined with the signature crisp popping sensation, it offers an exciting, playful twist that leaves a sweet fruity aftertaste.
