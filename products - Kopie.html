Product Description

PUC PUC is a premium carbonated popping candy designed to deliver an interactive and sensory confectionery experience. Manufactured using advanced food-processing technology, pressurized carbon dioxide gas molecules are trapped within the solid sugar structure. Upon contact with moisture on the tongue, the candy dissolves instantly, releasing the gas to create a distinctive, energetic popping and crackling sensation.
